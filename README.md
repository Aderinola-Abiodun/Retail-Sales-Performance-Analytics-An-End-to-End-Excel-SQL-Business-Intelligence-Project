# Retail-Sales-Performance-Analytics-An-End-to-End-Excel-SQL-Business-Intelligence-Project
📋 PROJECT OVERVIEW

Project Title: **Retail Sales Performance Analytics:** An End-to-End Excel & SQL Business Intelligence Project

**Tools Used:** Microsoft Excel | PostgreSQL

**Dataset: 4,280 sales transactions across 20 cities, 5 regions, 42 unique products, and 4,112 unique customers spanning 2020–2024.**

Business Context:
Nova Retail Group is a fast-growing retail and e-commerce company operating across multiple product categories and regions. Despite consistent sales growth, the executive team had grown concerned about declining profitability, inconsistent customer satisfaction, rising shipping costs, and increasing product returns. Making the challenge worse, operational data was being collected from multiple sales channels and warehouses simultaneously, resulting in messy records filled with missing values, duplicate entries, formatting errors, and inconsistent data types.

Management had no centralised reporting system and was relying on manual Excel reports that regularly produced conflicting numbers, making it impossible to trust the data enough to act on it confidently.

**My Task:**
As the Business Intelligence Analyst, I was tasked with transforming raw and unreliable operational data into accurate, structured, and actionable business intelligence. This project followed the complete data analytics lifecycle, beginning with data cleaning and standardization in Excel to ensure data quality and consistency. The cleaned dataset was then imported into PostgreSQL, where I designed the database and performed comprehensive exploratory data analysis through 50 SQL queries, progressing from fundamental data exploration to advanced analytical techniques such as window functions, common table expressions (CTEs), ranking, and customer segmentation. Finally, I developed an interactive Excel dashboard that provides executives with real-time visibility into key business metrics, enabling data-driven decision-making and delivering meaningful insights into sales performance, customer behavior, profitability, and operational efficiency.
