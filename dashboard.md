## Executive Dashboard

Following the data cleaning process in Excel and the exploratory data analysis in PostgreSQL, the final stage of this project was to develop an interactive executive dashboard that transforms complex transactional data into clear, actionable business insights.

The dashboard was designed with decision-makers in mind, providing a centralized view of the company's key performance indicators (KPIs) and enabling users to monitor sales performance, profitability, customer behavior, operational efficiency, and regional performance at a glance. Interactive filters and dynamic visualizations allow stakeholders to explore trends across different products, customer segments, regions, cities, and time periods, making it easier to identify opportunities, monitor performance, and support data-driven business decisions.

The dashboard presents key metrics such as total sales, total profit, average order value, customer satisfaction, shipping performance, product performance, and regional sales trends, offering a comprehensive overview of the company's operational and financial performance.
